How to turn a landscape video vertical

  1. Open your video

    Choose a landscape clip you want to post as a Short, Reel or TikTok.

  2. Reframe the shot

    Use Blur to keep the whole shot or Crop to fill the screen. Drag the image to move the frame or adjust it with the zoom control.

  3. Download the vertical clip

    Set the start and end if you want a shorter clip. The video exports at 720 × 1280 with the original audio.

Frequently asked questions

How do blur and crop differ?

Blur puts the original shot on top of a soft copy. Crop fills the canvas, which can leave parts of the shot outside the frame.

Can I move the frame?

Yes. Adjust the horizontal and vertical position to keep the subject in frame.

Is my file uploaded?

The file is processed in your browser. You don’t need an account.

How long does video export take?

It records while the clip plays, so export usually takes about as long as the clip. Choose MP4 with AAC audio or WebM.
